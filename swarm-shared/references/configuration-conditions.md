# Swarm Configuration Conditions

Use these as trigger-style rules rather than deterministic formulas.

## 1. Setup Intensity Conditions

### Required when
- `problem ambiguity` is `high`,
- or `rigor / stakes` is `high` and the framing itself may fail the task.

Then Phase 1 should explicitly consider:
- non-goals,
- hidden assumptions,
- candidate framings,
- decision target,
- or failed-search conditions.

### Recommended when
- the task mixes exploration and evaluation,
- or different framings would likely produce different swarms.

### Optional when
- the task is narrow, concrete, and already well-posed.

## 2. Team Topology Conditions

### Required when
- `rigor / stakes` is `high`,
- `creativity need` is `high`,
- or the task clearly risks one-sided synthesis.

Then the Moderator should make the critic / evangelist balance explicit rather than relying on an implicit default.

### Recommended when
- the task is mixed and one workflow preset does not obviously dominate.

### Optional when
- the task is small enough that the default topology already clearly fits.

## 3. Grounding Conditions

### Required when
- `grounding need` is `high`,
- or `rigor / stakes` is `high` and unsupported claims would materially damage the outcome.

Then the Moderator should treat early or timely `E` as a real expectation, not as a decorative option.

### Recommended when
- the task is likely to drift into elegant but weak synthesis without evidence.

### Optional when
- the task is genuinely exploratory and evidence is not yet the main discriminator.

## 4. Branching Conditions

### Required when
- `route branching budget` is clearly `narrow` or clearly `wide`,
- or the task has a history of either premature convergence or uncontrolled branching.

Then the Moderator should explicitly state whether the swarm is pruning aggressively or tolerating multiple live routes for longer.

### Recommended when
- `problem ambiguity` is `high`,
- or the task is mixed enough that route budget affects the whole process.

### Optional when
- the route budget is close to the default and no special branching pressure exists.

## 5. Persona Conditions

### Required when
- persona choice materially affects authority style, interpretive framing, or the usefulness of the discussion.

Then the Moderator should explicitly choose among:
- real named experts,
- abstract role labels,
- composite archetypes.

### Recommended when
- `creativity need` and `grounding need` pull in different directions,
- or when intellectual lineage or authority style is itself informative.

### Optional when
- personas are mostly cosmetic and role labels already suffice.

## 6. Closure Conditions

### Required when
- `closure strictness` is `high`,
- or `rigor / stakes` is `high`.

Then the swarm should explicitly decide:
- whether bounded uncertainty is acceptable,
- whether unresolved risks must remain visible,
- whether "answer not found" is an acceptable final state.

### Recommended when
- the task is mixed and different closure styles would change the recommendation quality.

### Optional when
- the task is low-stakes and a pragmatic answer with bounded caveats is clearly sufficient.

## Snapshot Visibility Conditions

### Recommended when
- the task is substantial,
- a preset is clearly being used,
- or one or two overrides materially affect swarm behavior.

### Optional when
- the task is small and default Swarm behavior is obviously adequate.

## Artifact Implication Conditions

### Recommended when
- the task is long-running,
- SSOT-sensitive,
- or likely to be resumed later.

### Optional when
- the output is clearly one-off and continuity is not important.
