# Option C - Sub-Group Discussion

Use `C` when the broad field is clear but one concrete sub-question needs deeper work.

## Best For

- ranking routes,
- reducing ambiguity on a single issue,
- turning a broad concern into a sharper target,
- letting the most relevant subset of experts do focused work.

## Output Shape

- Moderator reasoning
- exact subgroup question and participants
- participant selection rationale
- focused discussion
- narrowing deliverable
- compact route update
- next option selection

## Quality Bar

- the subgroup has a clear target,
- the scope is narrower than the previous round,
- subgroup participants are chosen for a specific reason,
- clarifications within the subgroup happen inline rather than becoming hidden debt,
- the subgroup ends with sharper scope, reduced ambiguity, a clearer discriminator, or a better-ranked route,
- the result changes route ranking, scope, or next action.

## Narrowing Contract

Use `C` to compress uncertainty around one scoped sub-question.

A good subgroup round should end with at least one of:
- a sharper scope,
- a reduced ambiguity set,
- a clearer discriminator,
- a ranked sub-route,
- or a more exact next move.

## Boundary

- Use `A` for broad multi-perspective exploration.
- Use `D` for route adjudication between competing positions.
- Use `X` for true adversarial cross-examination of incompatible positions.
- Use `C` for scoped refinement by the most relevant subset of experts.

## Failure Modes

- subgroup topic is still too broad,
- subgroup participants were chosen with no clear reason,
- subgroup discussion behaves like a smaller `A` instead of a narrowing tool,
- unresolved internal clarifications get dropped silently,
- `C` produces more framing instead of narrowing.
