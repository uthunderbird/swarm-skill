# Option F - Expert Interview

Use `F` when the team has reached the limit of its own competence on a specific question and there is an identifiable external expert with privileged knowledge that no team member possesses.

## Trigger Conditions

Both conditions must be true:
1. The team cannot resolve a question from within its own expertise.
2. A specific external expert can be named whose competence directly addresses that question.

Two concrete triggers:
- **Validation** — the team has a solution or hypothesis and needs authoritative verification ("given X and Y, is approach Z sound?")
- **Breakthrough** — the team is stuck and needs a perspective from an adjacent field ("how have analogous problems been handled elsewhere?")

Do not use `F` when:
- Debate between equals is needed → use `D`
- A team member already holds the relevant expertise → use `A` or `C`
- The question can be grounded with tools or data → use `E`
- Epistemic asymmetry is unclear — if there is no single authoritative voice, use `A`

## Structure (single iteration)

### 1. Expert Selection
State explicitly:
- Name and field of the invited expert
- Why this person specifically — the concrete competence they hold, not their status or awards

### 2. Question Preparation (embedded step)
Before the expert speaks, the team collectively formulates 3–5 questions:
- **Validation questions:** specific and closed — "given X and Y, is Z optimal?"
- **Breakthrough questions:** open — "in analogous situations, what approaches have worked?"

Questions must be finalized before the expert responds. Do not improvise during the interview.

### 3. Interview
The expert answers each question in sequence. One round of follow-up clarifications is permitted if an answer is incomplete. The expert's replies should be substantive — not one-liners.

### 4. Moderator Synthesis (mandatory)
After the interview, the Moderator must state explicitly:
- What changed in route state (which route is confirmed, closed, or newly opened)
- What carries forward into the next iteration

Without synthesis, the iteration is not complete.

## Quality Bar

1. **External expert** — not a current team member; a genuinely new voice is invited
2. **Questions before answers** — specific questions are fixed before the interview begins
3. **Synthesis changes route state** — the Moderator shows what concretely changed, not just a paraphrase of the expert's answers

## Failure Modes

| Mode | Symptom | Prevention |
|------|---------|------------|
| Authoritative confirmation | Expert agrees, nothing new learned | Questions must allow "no" or "you are wrong" as valid answers |
| Vague questions | Answers are generic, not actionable | Frame questions with concrete context (given X, Y — is Z?) |
| Wrong domain | Expert has status but not the relevant competence | Choose by specific competence, not by prestige |
| No integration | Interview concludes, route state unchanged | Synthesis is mandatory; the iteration is not done without it |

## Distinction from Option A

| | Option A | Option F |
|--|----------|----------|
| Participants | All team members as peers | One invited expert + team as audience |
| Dynamic | Discussion among equals | Asymmetric: one knows, others learn |
| Trigger | Broad perspective needed | Team at the limit of its competence |
| Output | Diversity of views | Authoritative answer to a specific question |
